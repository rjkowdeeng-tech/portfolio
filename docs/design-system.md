# Design System

## Purpose
Defines visual quality, aesthetics, and premium presentation.

## Principle
Typography is the primary design element.

## Commands
@design

## Rules
- Favor editorial layouts.
- Use strong hierarchy and generous whitespace.
- Maintain visual restraint.
- Prioritize consistency across spacing, typography, and color.
- Avoid dashboard aesthetics.
- Avoid excessive cards and borders.
- Every design decision should increase perceived quality.
- Move toward a founder-brand experience worth $20,000+ in design value.
