# Brand System

## Purpose
Defines who Ross John Dela Rosa is, what he represents, and how the website communicates value.

## Principle
Position Ross as a Builder, Operator, AI Workflow Designer, Automation Specialist, Vibe Coder, and Systems Thinker.

## Commands
@brand

## Rules
- Ross John Dela Rosa is the hero.
- Communicate leverage, systems, execution, and outcomes.
- Copy must be direct, confident, and minimal.
- Avoid buzzwords and generic freelancer language.
- Show value through results, not claims.
- Emphasize AI workflows, automation, operations, websites, and process optimization.
- The website is not a traditional portfolio.
